# What is in the Wren Street folder

- orders/ – wholesale-orders.csv: cafe orders from phone, web and email, with mixed date styles, cafe spellings and quantities in words.
- customers/ – cafes.csv: the six cafes we deliver to, with the right spelling, area and how many days they have to pay.
- prices/ – price-list.xlsx: shop and wholesale price for each product.
- invoices/ – 24 supplier invoices as PDFs (Fellside Dairy, Hollow Lane Eggs, Kraftline Packaging, Millbrook Mill).
- purchase-orders/ – purchase-orders.csv: what we ordered from suppliers and the agreed price. This is the record.
- recipes/ – one recipe for each of our 12 products, with ingredients and method.
- allergens/ – allergen-list.csv: each allergen and the words we look for in a recipe.
- labels/ – the shelf labels in the shop now: name, price, allergens, and a "Checked by" line.
- menu/ – menu.md: the menu on the shop wall, with shop prices.
- till/ – sales.csv: what was baked, sold and wasted each day, and the takings. This is the record.
- notes/ – owner-notes.md: Jess's rules for writing, dates, cafes and what never to do.
- out/ – where new files go: the Friday close outputs, the scripts in out/scripts/, and a couple of earlier notes.
