# What is in the bakery folder

- orders/ holds wholesale-orders.csv: 143 cafe orders from phone, web and email, with mixed date styles, cafe spellings and worded quantities.
- customers/ holds cafes.csv: the six cafes we deliver to, with the right spelling and how many days each takes to pay.
- prices/ holds price-list.xlsx: shop and wholesale prices for our 12 products.
- invoices/ holds 24 supplier invoices as PDFs from Fellside Dairy, Hollow Lane Eggs, Kraftline Packaging and Millbrook Mill.
- purchase-orders/ holds purchase-orders.csv: the 23 orders we placed with suppliers and the agreed prices. It is the record.
- recipes/ holds one recipe for each of the 12 products, with ingredients and method.
- allergens/ holds allergen-list.csv: the 7 allergens and the words we look for in recipes.
- labels/ holds the 12 shelf labels in the shop now.
- menu/ holds menu.md: the menu on the shop wall.
- till/ holds sales.csv, what sold, was wasted and taken each day from the till. It is the record.
- notes/ holds owner-notes.md: Jess's rules on writing, dates, cafes and what never to do.
- out/ is where new files go, and out/scripts/ holds the scripts that made them.
