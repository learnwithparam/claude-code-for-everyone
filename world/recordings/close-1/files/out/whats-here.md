# What is in the Wren Street folder

- orders: wholesale-orders.csv, cafe orders from phone, web and email, with mixed date styles, cafe spellings, word quantities and some repeated rows.
- customers: cafes.csv, the six cafes we deliver to, with the right spelling, area and how many days each has to pay.
- prices: price-list.xlsx, shop and wholesale prices for the twelve products.
- invoices: 24 supplier invoice PDFs from Fellside Dairy, Hollow Lane Eggs, Kraftline Packaging and Millbrook Mill.
- purchase-orders: purchase-orders.csv, what we ordered from suppliers and the agreed price. This is the record.
- recipes: one recipe for each of the twelve products, with ingredients and method.
- allergens: allergen-list.csv, the seven allergens and the words we look for in recipes.
- labels: the shelf labels in the shop now, one per product.
- menu: menu.md, the menu on the shop wall.
- till: sales.csv, what was baked, sold and wasted each day from 2026-10-06 to 2026-11-01. This is the record.
- notes: owner-notes.md, Jess's notes on how we write, dates, cafes and what never to do.
- out: new files go here. It already had safety.md and why-i-am-here.md.
